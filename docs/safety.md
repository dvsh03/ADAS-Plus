# ADAS-Plus Safety Guidelines

> [!CAUTION]
> ADAS-Plus is designed strictly for research, educational, and experimental purposes. It is NOT certified for autonomous vehicle driving or safety-critical automotive applications.

## 🛡️ Safety Considerations

1. **Fail-Safe Warning**: If sensor signals are lost or stream disconnects, visual warning indicator defaults to active alert state.
2. **Environmental Operational Limits**: Designed for daylight/indoor controlled testing. Thermal or low-light performance requires supplementary IR illumination.
3. **Driver Responsibility**: Vehicle operators must maintain full manual control at all times.
